      Donate a vehicle

      Your own estimate. Donated cars are usually sold at auction; the real amount is known only after the sale.

      Paperwork checklist
      • Title in your name, signed over as the program tells you.
      • Smog certificate if your area and the car's age require it (ask the program).
      • Tell the DMV you no longer own the car (California: Notice of Transfer and Release of Liability).
      • Remove plates if the program asks, and cancel your insurance after pickup.
      • Keep the written receipt from the program.

      Taxes: in the US, if the charity sells the car for more than $500, the deduction is generally limited to the sale price, and the charity sends you a form showing it. Not tax advice: ask a tax preparer.

      Ways to shrink the gap

      Try options

      Four ways to shrink the gap. Each card shows its effect on the home on the Calculator tab. Try one at a time.

      Rate buydown

      Pay points up front so the family gets a lower rate, which buys more house for the same payment.

      Paid up front by a seller, builder or program. 0 means no buydown.
      1 point = 1% of the loan. About 1 point per 0.25% is a common rule of thumb; lenders price this differently. Get a real quote.

      Community land trust

      The trust keeps the land, so the family buys only the house.

      Sample: $1,440,000 land line ÷ 12 homes on the fictional budget (doc 10).

      The trust keeps the land and the family buys only the house, so the land cost leaves the family's price. The land still has to be paid for, so it shows as a funding line. In return, resale prices are capped so the home stays affordable for the next family.

      Co-buyers

      Two households buy together, like relatives or friends.

      Sample: Herrera pay stubs, year-to-date average (docs 01b and 02).
      A relative or friend buying together. 0 means no co-buyer.

      Both co-buyers sign the loan and are responsible for it. Use a written co-ownership agreement that says what happens if one wants to sell.

      Shared equity loan

      A program pays part of the price now and gets the same share of the value back later, like UK Help to Buy.

      No monthly payment for a set time. When the family sells or refinances, they repay the same percent of the home's value then, not the original amount.

      UK Help to Buy lent up to 20% (40% in London). Dream For All has offered up to 20%, capped. Check current terms.
      Help to Buy charged no interest for 5 years, then fees started, so many owners remortgaged to repay it.

      Watch out: at the end of the no-payment period the family may need to remortgage at whatever rate applies then. On a resale-restricted home, value growth is capped, so the repayment and the family's gain are both smaller.
