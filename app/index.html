How the cap is worked out

Formula from CA Health & Safety Code 50052.5(b). Lower, extremely low and moderate income formulas were checked against published copies of the statute, not the official code site. The very low income formula is still unverified.

Mortgage rate

If the rate moves

The price is capped by income, so a higher rate buys a smaller loan and the gap grows by the same amount.
